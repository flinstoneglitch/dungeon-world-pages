QeNTROPY COMICS · DUNGEON WORLD

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective / last updated: August 20, 2026

The short version

Dungeon World is a single-player arcade game. It doesn't ask you to create an account, doesn't collect your name or contact details, and doesn't run its own servers that store information about you. Your game progress and purchase-unlock status are kept only on your own device.

The sections below explain the few places where outside services — used for ads and in-app purchases — are involved, and what they can see.

Information we don't collect

We (the developer of Dungeon World) do not collect, store, or have access to your name, email address, physical address, phone number, photos, contacts, or any other personally identifying information. There is no login, no account system, and no server run by us that logs your gameplay.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dungeon World offers an optional "watch an ad to continue" feature after a game over. These ads are served by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on these ads, Google's SDK may collect technical data such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, approximate location, and app interaction data.

Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), you'll be shown a consent prompt — powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — before any of this happens, and you can choose what you're comfortable sharing.

On iOS, Dungeon World also uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You'll see a system prompt asking whether the app can track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you can still watch ads to continue — you'll just see less personalized ones.

You can read Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchase

Dungeon World offers a one-time $1.99 "Remove Ads" purchase that unlocks unlimited continues and removes ad prompts. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. Your payment details (card number, billing address, Apple ID, etc.) are handled directly by Apple — the developer of Dungeon World never sees or stores that information. Apple's own privacy policy covers how they handle it: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Data stored on your device

Your purchase-unlock status, and general game state, are saved locally on your device only (using standard local device storage). This information stays on your phone and is not transmitted to us. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children's privacy

Dungeon World is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the email below and we'll address it.

Your choices

  • You can decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can manage or restore your in-app purchase through your Apple ID account settings, or via the "Restore Purchase" option inside the game.
  • Because we don't hold an account or profile about you, there's generally nothing on our end to request access to or deletion of — but if you have a question about this, reach out below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same web address with a new "last updated" date above.
